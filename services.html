The Heart of the Mediterranean

Malta: A Maritime Crossroads

Malta is a small island nation in the central Mediterranean Sea, serving as a vital maritime bridge between Europe, North Africa, and the Middle East. Renowned for its strategic location, the country is a major transshipment hub and a popular destination for cruise ships and superyachts. Ship Chandler Malta operates seamlessly across its key ports, ensuring every vessel calling at our shores is fully provisioned and ready for its next voyage.

Malta's Key Ports

Ship Chandler Malta provides rapid delivery and vessel supply services across the major maritime hubs of Malta. While we specialize in these primary ports, our logistics network allows us to coordinate deliveries to any anchorage or berth within the region.

Marsaxlokk

The primary commercial port of Malta, handling the majority of container, bulk, and cargo traffic. It is a crucial hub for transshipment and supply operations.

Valletta

The historic capital city and home to the Grand Harbour. A major destination for cruise liners, superyachts, and naval vessels, offering excellent provisioning opportunities.

Marsamxett

A natural harbour located north of Valletta, often used by ferries, yachts, and smaller commercial craft. It provides a quieter, yet accessible, supply point.

Mgarr

Located on the island of Gozo, this port is vital for inter-island transport and serves the growing maritime needs of the northern Maltese archipelago.
